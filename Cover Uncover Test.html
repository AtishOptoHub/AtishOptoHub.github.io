COVER TEST

Definition

  • Cover test is an objective clinical test of ocular alignment used to detect, differentiate and measure heterotropia (manifest deviation) and heterophoria (latent deviation).
  • It is considered the gold standard test for evaluating ocular misalignment (strabismus).
Figure 1: Objective Ocular Alignment Examination in Clinical Optometry

AIMS / PURPOSES OF COVER TEST

  • Detect presence of ocular deviation.
  • Differentiate heterophoria from heterotropia.
  • Determine direction of deviation.
  • Determine laterality (unilateral or alternating).
  • Assess fixation pattern.
  • Assess control of deviation.
  • Quantify angle of deviation when combined with prism.
  • Evaluate binocular vision status.

CLASSIFICATION OF COVER TEST

1. Cover Test (Single Cover Test)

Also called:

  • Unilateral Cover Test
  • Monocular Cover Test

Used mainly for detection of manifest deviation (tropia).

2. Cover-Uncover Test (CUT)

Used for:

  • Detection of heterotropia
  • Detection of heterophoria
  • Differentiation between tropia and phoria

3. Alternate Cover Test (ACT)

Used for:

  • Complete dissociation of fusion
  • Detection of total deviation
  • Measurement of heterophoria and heterotropia

4. Prism Cover Test (PCT)

Used for:

  • Quantification of deviation
  • Measurement in prism diopters (PD)

Includes:

  • a. Simultaneous Prism Cover Test (SPCT): Measures manifest deviation only.
  • b. Alternate Prism Cover Test (APCT): Measures total deviation (tropia + phoria).

REQUIREMENTS

Instruments

  • Occluder
  • Prism bar (horizontal and vertical)
  • Distance fixation target
  • Near fixation target
  • Trial frame/spectacles if prescribed

Test Distances

Distance Cover Test: 6 meters (20 feet)

Near Cover Test: 33–40 cm

Patient should wear habitual correction during testing whenever indicated.

BASIC PRINCIPLE

  • Under normal binocular vision, fusion maintains ocular alignment.
  • When one eye is covered:
    • Fusion is interrupted.
    • Deviated eye moves to its resting position.
    • Upon uncovering, eye refixates on target.
  • Observation of these movements allows identification of ocular deviation.

COVER TEST (SINGLE COVER TEST)

Definition

  • A test in which one eye is covered while observing movement of the fellow eye.
  • Used primarily to detect manifest strabismus (heterotropia).

Procedure

Step 1: Patient fixates on Distance target (6 m) or Near target (33–40 cm).

Step 2: Cover one eye for 1–2 seconds.

Step 3: Observe uncovered eye carefully.

Step 4: Remove cover.

Step 5: Repeat for opposite eye.

Interpretation

Normal Finding: No movement of uncovered eye. (Diagnosis: Orthotropia)

Abnormal Finding: Movement of uncovered eye indicates tropia.

DIRECTION OF REFIXATION MOVEMENT

  • Eye moves inward: Eye was deviated outward. → Diagnosis: Exotropia (XT)
  • Eye moves outward: Eye was deviated inward. → Diagnosis: Esotropia (ET)
  • Eye moves downward: Eye was elevated. → Diagnosis: Hypertropia
  • Eye moves upward: Eye was depressed. → Diagnosis: Hypotropia

COVER-UNCOVER TEST (CUT)

Definition

  • A test in which one eye is covered and then uncovered while movements of both eyes are observed.

Used to differentiate:

  • Orthotropia
  • Heterotropia
  • Heterophoria

PROCEDURE

Step 1: Patient fixates on accommodative target.

Step 2: Cover right eye for 2–3 seconds.

Step 3: Observe left eye.

Step 4: Uncover right eye.

Step 5: Observe right eye.

Step 6: Repeat for left eye.

INTERPRETATION OF COVER-UNCOVER TEST

A. Orthotropia

Findings: No movement of either eye on cover or on uncover.

B. Heterotropia (Manifest Deviation)

Observation: Uncovered eye moves to take fixation when fellow eye is covered.

Conclusion: Manifest squint present.

C. Heterophoria (Latent Deviation)

Observation: Covered eye drifts away under occlusion.

On uncovering: Eye refixates to target.

Conclusion: Latent deviation present.

TYPES OF HETEROPHORIA DETECTED

Horizontal

Exophoria: Eye drifts outward behind cover. Refixation movement: Inward.

Esophoria: Eye drifts inward behind cover. Refixation movement: Outward.

Vertical

Hyperphoria: Eye drifts upward. Refixation: Downward.

Hypophoria: Eye drifts downward. Refixation: Upward.

Torsional

Cyclophoria: Rotational latent deviation. Generally requires additional tests for confirmation.

ALTERNATE COVER TEST (ACT)

Definition

  • A dissociative cover test in which occluder is alternated rapidly between eyes without allowing binocular fusion.
  • Measures total deviation: Tropia + Phoria

PURPOSES

  • Detect latent deviation.
  • Determine maximum angle of deviation.
  • Measure total ocular misalignment.
  • Assess fusional control.

PROCEDURE

Step 1: Patient fixates target.

Step 2: Cover one eye.

Step 3: Transfer occluder rapidly to fellow eye.

Step 4: No binocular viewing allowed.

Step 5: Continue alternation several times.

Step 6: Observe movement of each eye when uncovered.

INTERPRETATION

Normal: No refixation movement. (Diagnosis: Orthophoria / Orthotropia)

Abnormal: Refixation movement present. Indicates ocular deviation.

INTERPRETATION OF MOVEMENTS

  • Inward refixation: Exophoria / Exotropia
  • Outward refixation: Esophoria / Esotropia
  • Downward refixation: Hyperdeviation
  • Upward refixation: Hypodeviation

PRISM COVER TEST (PCT)

Definition

  • A cover test performed with prisms to quantify angle of deviation.
  • Result expressed in: Prism Diopters (PD)

TYPES

1. Simultaneous Prism Cover Test (SPCT): Measures Manifest deviation only.

2. Alternate Prism Cover Test (APCT): Measures Total deviation (Tropia + Phoria).

PRISM SELECTION RULE

Deviation Prism Base Direction
Exotropia / Exophoria Base In (BI)
Esotropia / Esophoria Base Out (BO)
Hypertropia Base Down before hypertropic eye OR Base Up before fellow eye
Hypotropia Base Up before hypotropic eye OR Base Down before fellow eye

PROCEDURE OF ALTERNATE PRISM COVER TEST

Step 1: Perform alternate cover test.

Step 2: Identify direction of deviation.

Step 3: Place appropriate prism before eye.

Step 4: Repeat alternate cover test.

Step 5: Increase prism strength gradually.

Step 6: Find neutralization point.

Step 7: Record prism value.

END POINT (NEUTRALIZATION)

  • Neutralization achieved when no refixation movement is observed during alternate cover testing.
  • Recorded prism value equals deviation magnitude.

RECORDING OF RESULTS

Distance Examples:

  • 20 PD XT
  • 15 PD ET

Near Examples:

  • 8 PD XP
  • 6 PD EP

Vertical Examples:

  • 4 PD RHT
  • 3 PD LHoT

Abbreviation Key:

  • XT = Exotropia
  • ET = Esotropia
  • XP = Exophoria
  • EP = Esophoria
  • RHT = Right Hypertropia
  • LHoT = Left Hypotropia

NORMAL FINDINGS SUMMARY

  • Cover Test: No movement
  • Cover-Uncover Test: No tropia, No phoria recovery movement
  • Alternate Cover Test: No refixation movement
  • Prism Cover Test: 0 PD deviation
  • Final Diagnosis: Orthotropia / Orthophoria

ADVANTAGES OF COVER TEST

  • Objective test.
  • Simple and inexpensive.
  • Detects small deviations.
  • Applicable at distance and near.
  • Gold standard for ocular alignment assessment.
  • Useful in all age groups.

LIMITATIONS

  • Depends on examiner skill.
  • Very small deviations may be missed.
  • Poor cooperation affects results.
  • Difficult in nystagmus and poor fixation.
  • Accuracy reduced in uncooperative children.
