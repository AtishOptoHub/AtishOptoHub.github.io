1. Cornea

The cornea is the transparent, anterior one-sixth of the outer fibrous coat of the eyeball.

Layers of Cornea (Anterior to Posterior)

  1. Epithelium: Stratified squamous non-keratinized epithelium. High regenerative capacity.
  2. Bowman's Layer: Acellular layer. Does NOT regenerate if damaged (leads to scar).
  3. Stroma: Makes up ~90% of corneal thickness. Composed of collagen fibrils.
  4. Dua's Layer: Pre-Descemet's layer (~15 microns thick, highly resistant).
  5. Descemet's Membrane: Basement membrane of endothelium. Can regenerate.
  6. Endothelium: Single layer of hexagonal cells. Pumps fluid out to maintain dehydration (deturgescence). Does NOT regenerate.

Clinical Points

  • Refractive Power: ~+43.00 D to +44.00 D (Major refractive surface of eye).
  • Average Thickness: ~540 µm central, ~700 µm peripheral.

2. Retina

The light-sensitive inner neural coat of the eye responsible for converting light into electrical signals.

10 Layers of Retina (Outer to Inner)

  1. Retinal Pigment Epithelium (RPE)
  2. Photoreceptor Layer (Rods & Cones)
  3. External Limiting Membrane (ELM)
  4. Outer Nuclear Layer (ONL)
  5. Outer Plexiform Layer (OPL)
  6. Inner Nuclear Layer (INL)
  7. Inner Plexiform Layer (IPL)
  8. Ganglion Cell Layer (GCL)
  9. Nerve Fiber Layer (NFL)
  10. Internal Limiting Membrane (ILM)

3. Extraocular Muscles (EOM)

Six muscles control the movement of each eye (4 Recti, 2 Obliques).

Actions & Innervation

  • Superior Rectus (CN III): Elevation (Primary), Intorsion, Adduction.
  • Inferior Rectus (CN III): Depression (Primary), Extorsion, Adduction.
  • Medial Rectus (CN III): Adduction (Primary).
  • Lateral Rectus (CN VI): Abduction (Primary).
  • Superior Oblique (CN IV): Intorsion (Primary), Depression, Abduction.
  • Inferior Oblique (CN III): Extorsion (Primary), Elevation, Abduction.

Mnemonic: LR6 (SO4) 3 — LR is supplied by 6th nerve, SO by 4th nerve, all others by 3rd nerve.
